## POTENCI

Portions of `caustic/potenci.py`, including numerical parameter tables and
prediction logic, are adapted from POTENCI v1.3:

- Project: <https://github.com/protein-nmr/POTENCI>
- Reference: Nielsen, J. T. & Mulder, F. A. A., *J. Biomol. NMR* **70**, 141-165
  (2018), <https://doi.org/10.1007/s10858-018-0166-5>
- Local changes: ported to the CAUSTIC Python API, reorganised into typed data
  structures and functions, and integrated with CAUSTIC's random-coil feature
  pipeline.
